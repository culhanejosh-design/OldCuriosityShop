Welcome to The Old Curiosity Shop

Old Curiosity Shop is an arts and crafts store specializing in handcrafted sea glass and driftwood art.

Our Story

From Porch Hobby to Passion Project

Our small craft business started as a hobby on an enclosed front porch in Schenectady, New York. We made small gifts for family and friends for holidays, birthdays, and other special celebrations.

We always welcomed family members, children, and grandbabies to help us. As their interest grew, they were always excited to join in. It truly became a family affair.

A Growing Demand for Heartfelt Creations

As the years went by, we began noticing a genuine market for our family’s unique creations. People asked us to craft pieces using their own sentimental items, including dried flowers from weddings, funerals, and other cherished events.

Ready for the World

We heard the calling loud and clear. We established our business, created our website, and prepared to share our work beyond our local markets. Now, we are ready to share our handcrafted creations with the world.
